chase madulara — percussion, electronics · daniel flores — electric guitar, pedalboard · yvette mushimiyimana — vocals, delay · jarrett thompson — drumset, drum sampler · hugo flores garcía — electric bass, electronics, bird sounds · camilla forte — photos and album art
