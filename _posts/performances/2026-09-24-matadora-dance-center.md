made the sound world for MATADORA, INSÉKTA's new latine futurist dance theater work, together with samuel osorio. we spent the summer improvising in the studio, cut those jams into chapters, and turned them into a show score that ran out of ableton live. world premiere at the dance center of columbia college chicago, sept 24–26.
