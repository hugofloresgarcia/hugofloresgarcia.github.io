got to demo project sound bender on the main stage at [adobe max sneaks](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/11/11/adobe-max-sneaks) in miami beach, nov 11, hosted by terry crews (kelsey slay brought me up). it grew out of my long-running obsession with sound morphing, and it's what i've been building at adobe research with a big team this past year.

you describe a sound in a text prompt and sound bender scoops it right out of a finished mix. then you shape it on a sound canvas: four sound ideas, one in each corner, and a whole space of variations in between that you can move through in real time, more like playing an instrument than typing into a text box. record a path through the canvas and the sound stays synced to your video.

terry's review: "oh my foley artist."
